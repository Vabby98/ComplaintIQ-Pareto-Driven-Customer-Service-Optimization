# ComplaintIQ: Pareto-Driven Customer Service Optimization

A data-driven Pareto analysis project focused on identifying key complaint drivers and optimizing customer service efforts using Python and advanced visualizations.

---

## 📌 Project Overview
Customer service efficiency is critical in e-commerce—rising complaint volumes affect brand reputation and customer retention. **ComplaintIQ** performs Pareto analysis on complaint data to:

- Identify top complaint categories and drivers  
- Highlight the high-impact 20% of issues that create ~80% of complaints  
- Provide prioritized recommendations for operations, product and support teams  

---

## 🎯 Key Objectives
- Perform Pareto Analysis on complaint reasons  
- Rank complaints by frequency and impact  
- Identify product categories with the most issues  
- Visualize complaint trends for actionable insights  
- Deliver clear recommendations to reduce overall complaint volume  

---

## 📊 Techniques & Features
- Frequency distribution of complaints  
- Pareto chart (frequency vs. cumulative %)  
- Category-wise breakdown of issues  
- Focus on the top 20% contributors to reduce 80% of complaints  
- Recommendations for operations, product, and support teams  

---

## 🧰 Tools & Libraries Used
- **Python** (Google Colab Notebook)  
- **Pandas** – Data wrangling  
- **Matplotlib & Seaborn** – Visualizations  
- **Pareto Analysis logic**  
- **Data Cleaning and Grouping Techniques**
